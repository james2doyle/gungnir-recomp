## Steps

1. Install the APK and launch `com.gungnir.recomp/.GungnirActivity`.
2. Wait for the storage check and the publisher screens to finish.
3. At the title menu, touch Cross.
4. At the difficulty selection, touch the down sector of the on-screen d-pad.
5. Touch Cross to confirm.
